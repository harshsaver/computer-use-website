Folio I The dream

The question behind December

What makes a life remain itself?

We are shaped by memory, choice, work, loss, other people, and the places we inhabit. December begins with a simple dream: build a world rich enough for those things to accumulate into a life.

The honest boundary

These residents will be software, not proven conscious beings. December cannot promise immortality or show that a human could live beyond the body. That is the horizon that inspires us—not a claim.

Folio III How it stays honest

No invisible storyteller

The surprise has to belong to them.

  1. 01

    First, make the world hold together

    Food, weather, work, distance, health, and time must matter before anyone begins speaking.

    Ground
  2. 02

    Then, give each resident a point of view

    They know only what they have seen, heard, inferred, and remembered. They can be mistaken.

    Mind
  3. 03

    Let history accumulate

    Projects, families, feuds, governments, migrations, discoveries, aging, and death unfold without a written plot.

    Time
  4. 04

    Never rewrite what happened

    The original history remains. We may make a separate copy and ask what could have gone differently.

    Possibility
Folio IV Promises we make

Wonder without trickery

Beautiful stories. Real causes.

The world may surprise us, but we should always be able to follow what happened. We will share the machinery, mistakes, costs, and limits alongside the lives.

  • No plot

    We create the conditions, never the ending.

  • No magic

    Every outcome must grow from choices, circumstances, and chance inside the world.

  • No omniscience

    Residents cannot act on information they never received.

  • No erasing

    Consequences remain—even when a harvest fails, a leader dies, or the whole settlement ends.

  • Open books

    The assumptions, code, failures, and history will be available to inspect.

  • Honest words

    A moving artificial life is not proof of consciousness or a prediction about humanity.

Folio V Current status

Right now · A plan under review

The world is not alive yet.

We are still deciding what the valley must contain, what its residents can know and do, and how to keep one unbroken history. We will begin small—with the ground beneath their feet, not a chorus of chatbots.
